# QUEUE-MMS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

QUEUE-MMS is a queue management service for Central Philippine Adventist College offices. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you create an account or use the queue service:

- Name, email address, contact details, and account role.
- Queue requests, ticket numbers, selected services, status changes, and timestamps.
- Office and service assignments for staff and administrators.
- Technical information needed to keep the application secure and functional, such as session and security data.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Issue, display, and track queue tickets.
- Notify users about queue status and service activity.
- Give staff access to the office and services assigned to them.
- Produce operational reports and audit records.
- Protect the application against misuse and maintain system security.

## Sharing and Access

Access to personal information is limited to authorized administrators, assigned staff, and the user’s relevant office workflow. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

## Retention and Security

Queue and audit records may be retained for operational, reporting, and accountability purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices

You may request correction of inaccurate account information through an administrator. You may also ask an administrator about account deletion or data retention, subject to active queue records, legal obligations, and institutional requirements.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the QUEUE-MMS administrator through the Central Philippine Adventist College office responsible for the service.
