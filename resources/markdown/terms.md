# QUEUE-MMS Terms of Use

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

These terms govern access to QUEUE-MMS, the queue management service used by Central Philippine Adventist College offices. By creating an account or using the service, you agree to follow these terms and applicable institutional policies.

## Acceptable Use

You agree to:

- Provide accurate account information.
- Keep your password and account access secure.
- Use queue tickets and services for their intended institutional purpose.
- Follow staff instructions and office procedures.
- Avoid impersonation, abuse, interference, unauthorized access, or attempts to disrupt the service.

## Queue Tickets

Queue tickets are issued for available services and may be subject to office hours, capacity, and staff actions. A ticket does not guarantee immediate service. Users must monitor their ticket status and follow the instructions shown by the application or provided by staff.

## Accounts and Staff Access

Users are responsible for activity performed through their accounts. Staff and administrator access is limited by assigned roles and offices. Do not share access or attempt to use another person’s account.

## Service Availability

QUEUE-MMS may be updated, temporarily unavailable, or changed to support maintenance, security, or institutional needs. Queue status and estimated wait information are provided for operational convenience and may change as staff process tickets.

## Content and Records

Users must not submit misleading, abusive, or unlawful information. Queue, transaction, and audit records may be retained to operate the service, resolve disputes, and support institutional reporting.

## Suspension and Termination

Access may be suspended or terminated when an account violates these terms, creates a security risk, or is no longer authorized to use the service.

## Changes

These terms may be updated when the service or institutional requirements change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.

## Contact

For questions about these terms or a queue service, contact the QUEUE-MMS administrator or the relevant Central Philippine Adventist College office.
